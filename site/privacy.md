# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Tilt (on the App Store as "Tilt: Smart Blinds") controls motorized window blinds through Tilt controllers and a Tilt Hub in your home. It is built so that your information
stays with you.

## What Tilt keeps, and where

- **On your iPhone or iPad:** your Tilt Hub's address and the keys that secure your link to it and to your controllers (in the iOS Keychain), your
  display name, your scenes and your settings.
- **On your Tilt Hub, in your home:** the keys of the phones that may use your blinds, a backup of each controller's
  settings (so a controller can be replaced), and the pairing details for Apple Home and Alexa if you add them.
- **On your Tilt controller, in your home:** the names of the phones that may use it, your blinds' names and positions,
  your schedules, and, if you set it, a rough location (to about a kilometer) used only to work out sunrise and sunset
  for schedules.
- **In Apple's iCloud (Tilt's CloudKit container):** when someone asks to join your blinds, a short note with their
  display name and the room's name, so the owners' phones can be told; and when an owner removes a phone, a note that
  only the removed phone can read. These notes contain no keys, addresses or locations, and are deleted when the request
  is answered.

## What Tilt does not do

- No accounts and no servers of our own: your iPhone talks to your Tilt Hub over your home Wi-Fi, and the hub talks to your controllers over Thread, a low-power home radio network.
- No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
- Your location is never sent anywhere except your own hub and controllers.

## Your name

Tilt asks for your name once, with Sign in with Apple, so the people you share blinds with see who you are. You can
change it any time in Settings, Display Name. Tilt does not receive your email address or your photo.

## Apple Home and Alexa

If you add your Tilt Hub to Apple Home or to Alexa, that app handles the connection under its maker's own privacy
policy (Apple's or Amazon's). Tilt sends them only what they need to show and move your blinds.

## Deleting your information

Remove a controller from Tilt in its settings, or delete the app, to remove what Tilt keeps on your device. An owner
can remove any phone. Requests in iCloud are removed when answered or taken back.
