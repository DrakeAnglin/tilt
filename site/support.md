# Support

Have a question or found a problem with Tilt? [Open an issue]({{ site.issues }}) and describe what happened. Please
include your iPhone model, the version of Tilt (Settings, About) and the controller's firmware (a room's settings,
About).

## Common questions

**The app says a blind is not reachable.** Your iPhone talks to the Tilt Hub over your home Wi-Fi, and the hub talks
to the controllers over Thread. Check that your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi as the hub, that the hub is plugged in,
and that the controller's battery is charged.

**Someone else needs to control the blinds.** On your Wi-Fi, they open Tilt, tap Add, and ask to join. You approve
them in the room's settings, as a Guest (can open and close the blinds, but not change settings) or as an Owner.

**No owner can approve me anymore.** On the waiting card, tap "No owner left?" and turn the blind's wand as the app
shows. That phone becomes an owner.

**Trying Tilt without a controller.** On the first screen, tap "No controller yet? Try the Demo".
