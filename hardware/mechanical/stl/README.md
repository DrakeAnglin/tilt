Gear tips are ~0.74 mm wide (shortened 0.8 m addendum), so they print as two clean lines.

## What this print checks
- Spline socket clearance (from the test cups)
- Gear mesh at 15.1 mm centers (0.1 mm extra for printed gears), backlash 0.15 mm
- Worm and worm wheel run freely in the printed bearings
- N20 fits the pocket; the gearbox screws line up (assumed 9 mm spacing, M1.6)
- Overall fit in the rail; the wand loop exits through the rail floor at 75°

## Assumptions to check on your parts
- N20 without encoder: gearbox 9 mm + motor 15 mm long, 12 × 10 mm, shaft 3 mm D (2.5 across the flat)
- Worm spline ridges about 1.1 mm wide (the test cups show if the keys are too wide or narrow)
