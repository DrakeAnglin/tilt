# Print files (v0.3, ready to test)

Two separate units, both press-fit between the rail walls:
1. **Tilter**: replaces the factory tilter, keeps the factory worm + worm wheel, adds the N20 drive.
2. **Sensor module**: anywhere along the tilt rod (the rod passes straight through, any length). Geared AS5600
   angle sensor.

Generated by [`fusion/TiltParts`](../fusion/TiltParts/TiltParts.py) and checked with
[`tools/fusion_verify.py`](../../../tools/fusion_verify.py) in Fusion before export:
- **Interference:** none between any printed part, the factory parts, the motor, the rod, the board or the rail.
- **Tilter gears:** zero overlap at mid-backlash, 3.4° backlash, no contact through a full adapter revolution (16 positions).
- **Sensor gears:** zero overlap, 4.3° backlash, no contact through a full rod-gear revolution (12 positions).
- **Meshes:** every STL is closed (0 non-manifold edges) and matches its Fusion body.

**Printing the tilter halves:** both print **on their backs**, outer face on the bed and split face up. The worm groove,
bearing seats and gear pockets are then the top of the print: crisp and true, with no first-layer squish on surfaces the
worm and gears run against, and no supports. The only overhang is the small notch-filling block at one corner (a ~2 mm
ledge). See [main half](../../docs/renders/print-case-right.png) and [lid](../../docs/renders/print-case-left.png).

**Motor mount:** there's no motor tower. The main half is 3 mm thicker (11 mm), and the N20 gearbox sits 4 mm deep in a
rectangular socket in its back face. That stops it rotating, and 2 × M1.6 screws hold its face against the wall. The
rest of the motor sticks out free inside the rail. See [back face](../../docs/renders/case-right-back.png).

**Settings:** PETG (cases), PETG or PLA+ (gears), 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12 mm layers for the gears and 0.16–0.2 mm for
the rest, 4 walls, 30–40 % infill, **no supports**. 45° helical teeth print as a 45° overhang. The cases are
solid (no lightening pockets, so there are no internal bridges).

## Envelope
- **Tilter:** inside the 55.5 × 36.5 mm rail except a dome over the worm's top end, which rises 2.1 mm into the open slot
  between the rail's top lips (the window frame is 6.5 mm above). Length along the rod: −8.3 to +31 mm around the
  tilter plane (the motor pod is on the tilt-drum side).
- **Sensor module:** 14 mm thick plus a 3.6 mm board frame, fully inside the rail. Place it anywhere on the rod where
  there's ~18 mm free between tilt drums or past the tilter's motor pod.
